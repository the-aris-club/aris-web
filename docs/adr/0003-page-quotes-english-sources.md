# The page quotes English sources and translates nothing

Every line of copy on the page is reproduced word for word from a document the club already maintains in English, and each block names its source file. Nothing is translated.

The club's documents are split across two languages with no rule saying which applies to what: the charter, governance and decision records are Vietnamese, while job descriptions, the Community Terms, server rules and the recruitment form are English and declare English canonical. Translating the Vietnamese material for the page would produce a second version of a governance document maintained in a different repository, which is the failure mode the club's own `job-description/README.md` warns about, and which nothing would keep in sync.

The cost is stated plainly rather than paid quietly: the page never mentions the club's three priority domains, the six charter principles or the L0-L5 learning ladder, because all three exist only in Vietnamese. Those are the largest gaps in the page, and they are content gaps rather than defects. Closing one means the club publishes an English original; it does not mean someone here writes a translation.

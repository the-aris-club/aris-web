# No claim of HCMIU recognition, and no disclaimer

The public page carries no statement about HCMIU recognition, and no banner disclaiming it either. It is written as though the club is established, because it is only ever published after the competent authority confirms the club. The prohibition is satisfied by omission rather than by a disclaimer.

The charter forbids claiming recognition, and separately forbids using the HCMIU name, logo, colours or imagery without written permission. A disclaimer does not satisfy either rule; only making no claim does. The earlier plan to put a "not yet an official club" banner above the fold was dropped because it concedes a framing the page does not need: nothing on the page ever says the club is recognised, so there is nothing to walk back.

This is invisible in the code, which is why it is recorded here. A future editor adding a "students of HCMIU" line, a university logo, or a partnership line would break a rule that no test in this repository would catch.
